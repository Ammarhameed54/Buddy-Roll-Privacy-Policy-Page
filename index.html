Buddy Roll helps you turn your photos into a personal monthly newsletter that you send by email to friends and family.

By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

1. Information we collect

Information you provide

  • Account information: your name, email address, and password (if you sign up with email). Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication and are never visible to us.
  • Sign in with Google or Apple: if you choose these options, we receive the basic profile information the provider shares with us, such as your name and email address. If you use Apple’s “Hide My Email” feature, we receive a private relay email address instead.
  • Recipient (subscriber) information: the names and email addresses of people you add to receive your newsletter, along with their subscription status and time zone settings used to schedule delivery.
  • Newsletter content: newsletter titles, introduction text, photo captions, category and mood tags, photo order, and scheduling and delivery status.
  • Invite and gift codes: codes you create, share, or redeem, and the associated plan benefits.
  • Communications: any information you include when you contact us for support.

Information collected automatically

  • Push notification token and time zone: a device token from Firebase Cloud Messaging so we can send you reminders and app notifications, and your device’s time zone offset so reminders arrive at the right local time. The token is removed from your account when you sign out.
  • Photo dates: to organize photos by month, the App reads the date a photo was taken from the photo’s metadata. This happens on your device. We do not collect your photos’ location data.
  • Technical and diagnostic information: our service providers (such as Google Firebase) may automatically process limited technical data, such as IP address, device type, operating system version, and server logs, as needed to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the service.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, browsing history, or financial information. We do not use advertising identifiers and do not track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

2. How we handle your photos

Your privacy matters most when it comes to your photos, so we designed Buddy Roll to keep them on your device wherever possible:

  • Your photo library stays on your device. We do not upload, copy, or store your photo library on our servers. You choose which individual photos to include.
  • Google Photos: if you choose to import from Google Photos, the App uses Google’s Photos Picker with read-only access limited to the specific photos you select. We do not access the rest of your Google Photos library.
  • AI-generated captions and categories: when you use the App’s AI features, a compressed copy of each photo you select (and, for the newsletter introduction, related captions and notes) is sent securely to our AI provider, Anthropic, to generate suggested captions, categories, mood tags, and introduction text. The photo is processed in memory to return the result and is not stored by Buddy Roll. Under Anthropic’s commercial terms, content sent through its API is not used to train its models.
  • Sending a newsletter: when you send a newsletter, the photos you selected are transmitted securely to our servers, embedded directly into the email, delivered to your chosen recipients, and then immediately discarded. We do not save photo files in our database or cloud storage.
  • What we do store: the text that goes with your newsletter (captions, tags, ordering, and introduction) so you can keep editing your draft across sessions.

Please remember that once a newsletter is delivered, the recipients’ copies are held by their own email providers and are outside our control.

3. How we use information

We use the information described above only to:

  • Create and manage your account and authenticate you;
  • Build, schedule, and deliver your newsletters to the recipients you choose;
  • Generate AI-suggested captions, categories, and introduction text when you request them;
  • Send you reminders and in-app notifications (for example, when it is time to prepare your monthly newsletter);
  • Process unsubscribe requests from your recipients;
  • Manage plan limits, invites, and gift code redemptions;
  • Provide support and respond to your requests;
  • Maintain the security, integrity, and reliability of the App, and prevent fraud and abuse; and
  • Comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information, share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, or use it to show you ads.

Recipients can also contact us directly to request removal of their information.

5. Device permissions

  • Photos / media library: to let you choose photos for your newsletter. You can grant limited access to selected photos only.
  • Camera (where offered): to take a new photo to include in your newsletter.
  • Notifications: to send reminders and updates.

You can change or revoke these permissions at any time in your device’s settings. Some features may not work without the relevant permission.

6. Data retention

  • We keep your account information, subscriber list, and newsletter text for as long as your account is active.
  • Photo files are not retained by us; they are discarded immediately after AI processing or email delivery.
  • Draft information saved locally on your device stays there until you delete it, sign out, or uninstall the App.
  • When you delete your account, we delete your associated data as described below. Limited information may remain in backups or service provider logs for a short period before it is automatically deleted, or longer where required by law.

7. Account and data deletion

You can delete your account at any time directly in the App: open Settings and choose Delete Account & Data. This permanently deletes your Buddy Roll account and the data associated with it, including your profile, subscriber list, newsletters and drafts, notifications, and the invite codes you created. If you signed in with Apple, Buddy Roll’s access to your Apple ID is also revoked.

If you can no longer access the App, you can request account deletion by emailing us at buddy@buddyrollapp.com from the email address associated with your account. We will process your request within 30 days.

If you signed in with Apple, you can also stop using Sign in with Apple for Buddy Roll from your Apple ID settings.

You can also turn off push notifications in your device settings, edit your profile and recipient list in the App, and choose not to use the AI features.
